# Himmel Pharmaceutical Sales Management System — User Guide

## Overview
Welcome to the Himmel Pharmaceutical Sales Management System. This guide assists sales representatives and managers in performing daily operational tasks.

---

## 1. Executive Dashboard
- **Analytics & KPIs**: View live total revenue, order metrics, active doctor directory metrics, and target progress.
- **Quick Shortcuts**: Access sales entry, product catalog, doctor management, and reports directly from the dashboard.

---

## 2. Product Master & Sales Operations
- **Product Directory**: Search and filter medical products by division, group, and status.
- **Sales Entry Workflow**:
  1. Select Area and Representative.
  2. Choose Doctor and Medical Institution.
  3. Add products and quantities (Trade Price and discounts auto-calculated).
  4. Save as **Draft** or **Submit for Approval**.

---

## 3. Order Lifecycle Management
- **Draft**: Work in progress order. Can be edited or deleted.
- **Pending**: Submitted order awaiting manager approval.
- **Approved / Completed**: Approved order contributing to sales targets and analytics.
- **Cancelled**: Order cancelled with recorded cancellation audit notes.
